Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how LoanCompass ("we", "us", "this site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

The numbers you enter stay on your device

The loan calculator runs entirely in your web browser with JavaScript. The home price, loan amount, rate, down payment, and any other inputs you type are never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never linked to you. You can use the tool without entering any personal information at all.

Information we may collect automatically

Like most websites, we may receive standard technical data through cookies and analytics, such as:

  • Usage data: pages visited, time on page, referring site, approximate region, device and browser type — aggregated to understand which features are useful.
  • Cookies: small files set by this site and by third-party services (Google Analytics, and advertising partners) to make analytics and ads work.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and how the site is used. It collects standard log and behaviour data and reports it in aggregate. We do not tie this data to your identity, and the calculator itself sends no personal data to analytics.

To learn more about how Google uses data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms page.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for behavioural advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Ads never influence the calculator's maths, and we do not share your inputs with advertisers.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not stop you from using the calculator, though it may limit ad personalisation and some analytics.

Third-party links

This site may link to external resources. We don't control those sites and aren't responsible for their privacy practices. Review each site's policy before providing information.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features or services change, and will revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or how we handle data? Reach us via the contact page.
