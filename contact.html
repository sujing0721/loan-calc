What we can help with

  • Calculation issues — if a result looks off or doesn't match a lender's quote in a way you can't explain, tell us the inputs and what you expected.
  • Feature requests — support for other currencies, bi-weekly mortgage payment modes, refinance break-even calculators, or downloadable amortization tables.
  • Corrections — if the PMI assumptions, default rates or explanations on the page are outdated, let us know and we'll update them.

What we can't do

We can't provide personal financial or lending advice, arrange loans for you, or interpret a specific lender's quote. For that, reach out to a licensed mortgage broker or financial professional — this site only offers general estimates.

Response time

LoanCompass is a small project run in spare time. We aim to reply within 5–7 business days. If it's urgent or you don't hear back, send a follow-up.
